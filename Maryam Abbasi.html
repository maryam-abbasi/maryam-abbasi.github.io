About

Maryam Abbasi is a researcher in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, holding a PhD in Informatics Engineering (University of Coimbra, 2019, Distinction and Honour). She holds a permanent position as Auxiliary Researcher at Santarém Polytechnic University, is an Integrated Researcher at the Centre for Research in Arts and Communication (CIAC – Pólo de Literacia Digital e Inclusão Social), and a collaborating researcher at the Laboratory of Artificial Neural Networks (LARN), CISUC, University of Coimbra.

Her research lies at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Bioinformatics, Health, and Behavioural Sciences, with particular focus on deep generative models, multi-objective reinforcement learning, transformer architectures, and explainable AI.

Research focus

Explainable & trustworthy ML

Classification, model comparison, threshold selection, and evaluation methods that researchers and clinicians can interpret and trust.

AI for health & bioinformatics

Readmission prevention, healthcare access, biomedical image analysis, and multi-omics applications.

Drug discovery & generative models

Molecular generation, reinforcement learning, and transformer-based design for antimicrobial peptides and small molecules.

Agentic AI & knowledge graphs

Research automation, knowledge graph construction, and secure, adversarially-robust large language models.
